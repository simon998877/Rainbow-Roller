Welcome to RR: Kinetic Support
Thank you for playing RR: Kinetic! We are dedicated to providing a smooth and relaxing physics experience. If you encounter any issues or have suggestions, please refer to the information below.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ):

• How do I reset the simulation?
Simply shake your device.

• The app is lagging, what should I do?
RR: Kinetic uses a high-performance physics engine. If you experience lag, try reducing the number of active spheres on the screen.

• Is my data safe?
Yes. RR: Kinetic does not collect or store any personal user data.
